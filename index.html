    How does this calculation work?
    • Cash is checked at the end of each month. Short cash gaps within a month are not included.
    • Interest is charged on debt at the start of the month. Unpaid interest adds to the debt.
    • Overhead stops when the work ends. The calculation runs until the last scheduled payment. Interest after that date is not included.
    • Starting cash reduces borrowing but does not add to profit. The model assumes borrowing is available when needed.
    • Scenarios apply from the start of the project. Materials already bought are not tracked separately. Cost changes apply once, not every month.
    • Extra months mean slower progress. A 2-month plan stretched to 4 months spreads each old month over two new months. Work and contract value stay the same, but overhead continues. Change the labour percentage if needed.
    • Tax, deductions and price adjustment payments are not included. Results depend on the plan you enter.
